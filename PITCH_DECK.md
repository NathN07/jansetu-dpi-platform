# 🏛️ JanSetu AI (जन-सेतु AI) — Pitch Deck (12 Slides)
### Build with AI: Code for Communities — Second Edition
**Track 01:** AI for Digital Public Infrastructure & Governance (Theme: Innovation)

---

## Slide 1: Title & Vision
* **Title:** JanSetu AI (जन-सेतु AI)
* **Tagline:** Multilingual Digital Public Infrastructure for Demand-Driven Governance & Autonomous Infrastructure Sanction
* **Mission:** Connecting 1.4 Billion Indian citizens directly to national infrastructure capital allocation.
* **Core Technology:** Google Gemini 3.8 Flash, Speech-to-Text, Translation, BigQuery, PM Gati Shakti GIS.
* **Certification:** Designed as a sovereign Open Digital Public Good (DPG).

---

## Slide 2: The Core Problem
* **Siloed & Fragmented Systems:** Citizen feedback lives scattered across paper petitions, local collectorates, and disconnected departmental portals.
* **The Vernacular & Literacy Divide:** Over 70% of rural citizens face literacy and English language barriers, unable to engage with legacy governance portals.
* **9 to 18 Month DPR Paralysis:** Bureaucratic gestation for formulating engineering Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) stalls urgent interventions while communities suffer.
* **Misaligned Public Spending:** Millions of rupees allocated based on political influence rather than acute grassroots demand and socio-economic deprivation.

---

## Slide 3: Citizen Reality in India
* **Case 1 (Bahraich, UP):** Broken drinking water pipeline leaves 12,000 villagers drinking contaminated water; 40 children hospitalized with waterborne illnesses.
* **Case 2 (Nabarangpur, Odisha):** Collapsed culvert during flash flood cuts off 6 tribal villages from schools and emergency 108 ambulances.
* **Case 3 (Dumka, Jharkhand):** Dilapidated primary health clinic ceiling collapse halts institutional deliveries for pregnant women.
* **Conclusion:** Citizens possess the ground truth, but governance infrastructure lacks the digital bridge to hear and act on it.

---

## Slide 4: The Solution — JanSetu AI
* **Jan-Vani (Voice of the People):** Citizen portal supporting voice-first input, photo inspection (Gemini Vision), and WhatsApp/IVR hotlines in 10+ Indian languages.
* **Shasan-Drishti (Geospatial Hotspot Map):** Clusters thousands of citizen distress records against NITI Aayog Aspirational Districts and PM Gati Shakti layers.
* **Autonomous DPR Engine:** Synthesizes engineering specifications, CPWD Bill of Quantities (BOQ), and budget allocations in under 48 hours.
* **Jan-Praman (Transparent Audit):** Cryptographic DPI tracking token ensuring end-to-end auditability and zero corruption leakages.

---

## Slide 5: Google AI Architecture
* **Gemini 3.8 Flash Multimodal:** Inspects citizen photos of broken roads, pipes, and roofs; computes hazard severity scores (1-10) and auto-classifies into central schemes.
* **Cloud Speech-to-Text & Translation:** Live voice transcription and bidirectional translation across Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Kannada, Odia, Gujarati, Punjabi.
* **Gemini Policy Copilot ("Shasan-Mitra"):** Strategic advisor answering real-time policy queries and simulating budget reallocations for Chief Ministers' War Rooms.
* **Google Cloud BigQuery:** High-throughput spatial telemetry analytics engine processing millions of citizen demand coordinates.

## Slide 6: Real-World Public Data Grounding
* **NITI Aayog Aspirational Districts:** Live integration of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) across 112 priority districts to ensure equity weighting.
* **PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan:** GIS Layer 14 mapping rural logistics corridors, waterways, and infrastructure rights-of-way.
* **data.gov.in & Census Indices:** Micro-level demographic indices (SC/ST percentage, BPL density, female labor participation).

## Slide 7: Multi-Criteria Algorithmic Fairness
* **Preventing Urban Bias:** Traditional portals favor affluent urban citizens with broadband. JanSetu AI levels the playing field using an open equity formula:
  $$\text{Priority Score} = 0.35 \times \text{Density} + 0.25 \times \text{Hazard} + 0.20 \times \text{Aspirational District} + 0.20 \times \text{Gati Shakti}$$
* **Impact:** Guarantees that a collapsed tribal bridge in Nabarangpur outranks a cosmetic sidewalk upgrade in a metro city.

---

## Slide 8: The Gamechanger — Autonomous DPR Formulation
* **Legacy Flow:** 270 days average gestation from citizen petition to financial sanction; heavy dependence on private consulting firms (₹2.5 Lakhs+ per DPR).
* **JanSetu Autonomous Flow:** Under 48 hours. Formulates official CPWD-compliant reports with:
  - Civil engineering scope (conforming to IRC/CPWD/IS codes)
  - Itemized financial BOQ in ₹ Lakhs & Crores
  - Central (60%) vs State (40%) cost sharing
  - 12-month execution milestones
  - Social Return on Investment (SROI) verification
