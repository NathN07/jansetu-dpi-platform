# 🎬 JanSetu AI — 3 to 5 Minute Demo Walkthrough Video Script
### Hackathon Presentation Guide for Judges: "Build with AI: Code for Communities — Second Edition"

---

## ⏱️ Video Structure Overview:
- **0:00 - 0:45:** The Problem & Vision (Why JanSetu AI matters for 1.4B Indians)
- **0:45 - 1:45:** Citizen Voice & Vision Ingestion (Jan-Vani live demo)
- **1:45 - 2:45:** Geospatial Command Center & Hotspots (Shasan-Drishti)
- **2:45 - 3:45:** Autonomous AI DPR Formulation & Project Sanction
- **3:45 - 4:30:** WhatsApp Bot Simulation & BigQuery Analytics
- **4:30 - 5:00:** Conclusion, Digital Public Good Impact & Next Steps

### Segment 1: The Problem & Introduction (0:00 - 0:45)
* **Visual:** Open JanSetu AI homepage with Indian Tricolor accents and Digital Public Good badge.
* **Narrator:**
  > "Namaste! Welcome to **JanSetu AI (जन-सेतु AI)**, our submission for Track 1: AI for Digital Public Infrastructure and Governance in Build with AI: Second Edition.
  > Across India, 1.4 billion citizens encounter daily infrastructure failures: broken drinking water pipelines, collapsed rural bridge culverts, or dilapidated school sanitation blocks.
  > But today, citizen grievances are trapped in fragmented silos, while drafting government Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) takes 9 to 18 months. Public spending remains disconnected from real human suffering.
  > JanSetu AI changes this forever by using **Google AI** to convert raw citizen voice, photo, and messaging complaints into Cabinet-sanctioned infrastructure projects in under 48 hours."

---

### Segment 2: Citizen Voice & Multimodal Vision Ingestion (0:45 - 1:45)
* **Visual:** Switch to **Citizen Portal ("Jan-Vani")**.
* **Action:**
  1. Click on the **"💧 Arsenic Water Pipe Burst (UP)"** demo preset or click the **Mic button** to speak.
  2. Show Gemini Vision inspection analyzing the broken pipe/road photo, outputting defect classification, hazard index (9.4/10), and target ministry (*Ministry of Jal Shakti / Jal Jeevan Mission*).
  3. Click **"Submit Grievance to National DPI"**.
  4. Watch confetti fire and point to the newly generated DPI Token: **`JS-UP-2026-8821`**.
* **Narrator:**
  > "Notice how effortless this is for a citizen. A rural farmer doesn't need to speak English or type a formal form. They can simply speak in Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, or snap a photo.
  > Powered by **Gemini 3.8 Flash Multimodal**, JanSetu immediately verifies the structural damage, computes a hazard severity score, and registers the demand as a sovereign Digital Public Good with a cryptographic tracking token."

---

### Segment 3: National Geospatial Hotspot Command Center (1:45 - 2:45)
* **Visual:** Switch to **National Hotspot Map ("Shasan-Drishti")**.
* **Action:**
  1. Pan across the interactive India Leaflet map.
  2. Toggle map layers: *★ NITI Aspirational Districts* and *⚡ Gati Shakti High Gaps*.
  3. Click on the glowing red marker for **Bahraich, Uttar Pradesh**.
  4. Highlight the sidebar metrics: Deficit Score: 89/100, 1,420 total demands, funding gap of ₹132.5 Crore.
  5. Click the button: **"Generate Autonomous AI DPR for Bahraich"**.
* **Narrator:**
  > "Now, let's step into the shoes of a District Magistrate or Central Ministry policymaker.
  > On our geospatial command center, JanSetu AI clusters thousands of citizen telemetry pings against **NITI Aayog Aspirational District Indicators** and **PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan Layer 14**.
  > Rather than relying on political lobbying, the AI surfaces acute demand hotspots where human suffering and infrastructure deficits converge. With one click, we can formulate an official DPR for Bahraich."

---

### Segment 4: Autonomous DPR Engine & Project Sanction (2:45 - 3:45)
* **Visual:** Automatically transitions to **AI DPR & Allocation Engine**.
* **Action:**
  1. Click **"Formulate Detailed Project Report (1-Click)"**.
  2. Show the newly formulated CPWD-compliant document:
     - Executive summary
     - Engineering scope of work
     - Financial Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with itemized costs in ₹ Lakhs & Crores (e.g. ₹14.8 Cr)
     - Central (60%) vs State (40%) share
     - 12-month milestone timeline
  3. Click **"Sanction Budget & Issue Administrative Approval"** — confetti triggers!
  4. Scroll to **"Shasan-Mitra" AI Policy Copilot** at the bottom and ask: *"Simulate a ₹100 Crore reallocation under PM Gati Shakti for eastern tribal districts."*
* **Narrator:**
  > "This is our platform's most revolutionary feature: the **Autonomous AI DPR Engine**.
  > Traditionally, drafting a project report with engineering specifications and line-item budgets takes consulting firms months and costs lakhs of rupees.
  > Here, Gemini 3.8 Flash drafts a complete CPWD-standard Detailed Project Report in seconds, itemizing pipes, solar microgrids, and IoT telemetry sensors.
  > Officials can immediately issue Administrative Sanction, cutting gestation by 95%."
